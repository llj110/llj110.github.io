About me关于我

I am a Ph.D. student at the Faculty of Science and Technology, University of Macau, supervised by Prof. Chi-Man Pun. I received my B.S. in Software Engineering from Southwest University in 2023.

My research focuses on multimodal learning, generative AI, trustworthy machine learning, and semi-supervised learning. My work explores how to learn from complementary information and uncertainty, with applications in medical image segmentation, knowledge graph completion, and robust visual understanding.

我目前在澳门大学科技学院攻读博士学位，导师为 Chi-Man Pun 教授。2023 年，我于西南大学获得软件工程学士学位。

我的研究兴趣包括多模态学习、生成式人工智能、可信机器学习与半监督学习。我的工作关注如何利用互补信息和不确定性提升学习效果，应用涵盖医学图像分割、知识图谱补全与鲁棒视觉理解。
