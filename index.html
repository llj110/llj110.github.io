About me关于我

I study multimodal learning and robust visual perception, with a focus on uncertainty modeling, semi-supervised learning, and test-time adaptation.

I am a Ph.D. student at the Faculty of Science and Technology, University of Macau, supervised by Prof. Chi-Man Pun. I received my B.S. in Software Engineering from Southwest University in 2023.

I study how to combine complementary information and adapt models when labels are scarce or data distributions change. My recent work spans online 3D reconstruction, cross-modal retrieval, medical image segmentation, and multimodal knowledge graph completion.

我的研究聚焦于多模态学习与鲁棒视觉感知，重点探索不确定性建模、半监督学习和测试时适应。

我目前在澳门大学科技学院攻读博士学位，导师为 Chi-Man Pun 教授。2023 年，我于西南大学获得软件工程学士学位。

我关注如何融合互补信息，并在标注有限或数据分布变化时提升模型的可靠性与适应能力。近期工作涵盖在线三维重建、跨模态检索、医学图像分割和多模态知识图谱补全。

  • Multimodal Learning
  • Uncertainty Modeling
  • Test-Time Adaptation
  • 3D Vision
